The Edge Band, Laure and Vyoma to light up The Tivoli on 2 December

Brisbane, mark your calendars. SarkarFest is back for its second year, and it’s bigger than ever.

Brisbane-based Sarkar Entertainment will bring SarkarFest 2026 to The Tivoli on 2 December. Legendary rock group The Edge Band, chart-topping rapper Laure and singer Vyoma will share one stage for one massive night.

Last time, fans didn’t wait. Sarkar Entertainment’s previous event sold out a full nine days before the show, and the organisers say this year is heading the same way.

“Tickets are moving fast,” said Sarkar Entertainment manager Saroj Singh Thakuri. He added that the company has built a strong track record of successful concerts and music events in Brisbane, and he’s urging fans to book early so they don’t miss out.

Ambition Education and Visa Services is the event’s major sponsor.

For Brisbane’s Nepali community, SarkarFest has quickly become a must-attend event. If last year is anything to go by, this year’s tickets won’t last long.

Date: 2 December 2026
