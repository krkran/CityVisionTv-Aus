Hamro Story: new episodes on the last Saturday of every month

Our series about Nepali people building their lives in Australia continues, with a new episode released on the last Saturday of each month.

By CityVision TV27 September 2026, 1:00pm · 1 min read
Share

Hamro Story shares the real stories of Nepali people who have made Australia home: the journeys that brought them here, the challenges along the way and the communities they have built.

New episodes are released on the last Saturday of every month on our YouTube channel and Facebook page.

Be part of the show

We are always looking for stories to tell. If you, or someone you know, has a story the community should hear, get in touch with a little about yourself and why you would like to be featured.
