Welcome to the new home of CityVision TV

News, shows and stories from the Nepali community in Australia, now in one place. Here is what you will find and how to stay in the loop.

CityVision TV has a new website. It is the place to find our latest stories, full episodes of our shows and updates from the Nepali community across Australia.

What you will find here

Our newsroom publishes news, community stories, culture and events. Every show now has its own page, so you can catch up on Hamro Story, Kurakani, Diaspora Talk, My Days in Nepal and more.

By Nepali, for Nepali. Our stories, told by our community.

Never miss an update

  • Tap the bell at the top of any page to see what is new since your last visit.
  • Visit Latest updates for every new story and episode as it happens.
  • Subscribe to our newsletter at the bottom of the page.
  • Follow us on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Spotify.

Share your story

Know someone doing something remarkable? Organising a community event? Send us a story tip and our team will be in touch.
