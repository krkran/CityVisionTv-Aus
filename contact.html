Common questions

How do I get featured on Hamro Story?

Send us a message with your name, a little about yourself and why you would like to be on the show. New episodes are released on the last Saturday of each month.

Can I submit a news story or tip?

Yes. Choose “Story tip or news submission” and tell us what happened, where and when. Include as much detail as you can and we will follow up.

How do I advertise with CityVision TV?

We offer advertising and sponsorship across our website, YouTube and Facebook. Choose “Advertising and sponsorship” and tell us about your business and goals.

Can I list a community event?

Absolutely. Choose “Community event listing” and include the event name, date, time, location and a short description. We will add it to our Events section.

How do I appear on the Kurakani podcast?

Choose “Kurakani podcast enquiry” and tell us your story: your background, what you would like to talk about and your connection to the Nepali diaspora.
